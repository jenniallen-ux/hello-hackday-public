Alex's First Card

An interactive story that helps people new to credit build a credit strategy, instead of leaving them to work it out alone.

Try the latest prototype → Best on a phone. Takes about five minutes.

The idea

Someone gets their first credit card. Instead of reading terms they'll forget, they make six months of choices for Alex, a fictional first-time cardholder, and see what each one does to their balance, costs and credit file.

The thread running through it is credit utilisation: how much of your limit you use. Too little builds nothing. Too much counts against you. In between shows lenders you can manage credit.

How it came together

  1. 1
    A choose-your-own-adventureSix monthly decisions, a live dashboard and a "why this happened" tip after every choice.
  2. 2
    Utilisation became the threadNot using the card and maxing it out are both shown as mistakes, with a healthy range in the middle.
  3. 3
    From understanding to a planA companion flow, Build My Plan, turns the lesson into a commitment: choose a bill to move onto the card and how to pay it off.
  4. 4
    Tested with a real personOne session, four clear changes (below), all built the same afternoon.
  5. 5
    Three visual directionsCurrent, minimal and illustrative treatments explored in Figma. Minimal won: quiet while you decide, bold when you see the outcome.
  6. 6
    The minimal prototypeThe latest version, rebuilt in the chosen direction.

What testing taught us

Explain before you showUtilisation now starts with a question, so the indicator means something when it appears.
Let people compareYou can step back and try a different choice to see what would have happened.
Mirror real decisionsStatements offer the real options: pay in full, the minimum, or your own amount.
End with something practicalThe story finishes by setting up reminders, then leads into building your own plan.
